Where the site stands today

Eleven pages on Webflow. The business already has the right conversion plumbing: a Podium text-us bubble, click-to-call with Google Ads phone tracking, a LeadConnector booking calendar, and a contact form. What it lacks is a presentation that matches the wholesale credentials and custom work, and content that is consistent and complete.

What the rebuild fixes

  • Duplicated home sections. Temporary Ring, Lifetime Upgrade and Out-of-the-box Request each appear twice in the page (a desktop and a mobile copy).
  • Three founding dates. "Since 1969, 35+ years" on Home; "Since 1995" and "since 1999" on About. One story is needed.
  • Bare collection pages. Image grids with no names, specs, filters, lightbox or a way to ask about a piece.
  • Borrowed product photos. Many grid images trace by filename to Tiffany, Brilliant Earth, Clean Origin, Cartier, Blancpain and Amazon listings. The Watches page shows brands Romi may not sell.
  • Weight. The hero is a 24 KB stock crop while the stylesheet still references about 40 unused template photos.
  • Small errors. Currency set to USD; "pirces", "dissapointed", "finance" for fiancé, "SCHedule"; privacy policy names bespokediamonds.com.
02

Design direction

The jewellers that convert well online (VRAI, Mejuri, Brilliant Earth, Tiffany, and locally Dainty Diamond) follow one proven pattern: a quiet ivory ground, a single warm metal accent, a refined serif for headlines, generous space, photography that carries the page, and a "Book an appointment" action that never leaves the screen. We apply that pattern using Bespoke's own gold, sampled from the logo and deepened for contrast. This is the client's brand throughout, not Kim Consultant's.

Palette

Ivory#FAF7F1 · ground
Ink#141210 · text, footer
Champagne#D4B36A · accent, rules
Deep gold#9C7A3C · gold text
Warm stone#6F675C · secondary
Hairline#E4DCCD · dividers

Gold stays an accent: hairlines, eyebrows, hover states, one primary button. Dark ink sections (footer, the appointment band) carry champagne type so the page has rhythm without heavy fills.

Type

Italiana · eyebrows and wordmark echo
Custom diamonds, made for you.
Jost for body and interface: geometric and quiet, it sits well with the crown geometry of the logo. Cormorant Garamond carries headlines. Carat and millimetre figures use tabular numerals.

Layout and motion

  • Twelve-column grid, 1280 px maximum, consistent 88 px section rhythm.
  • Slim sticky header that turns solid on scroll. "Book appointment" is always visible; on mobile a bottom bar offers Call · Text · Book.
  • Motion follows the KC scroll playbook: fade-and-rise reveals, hover zoom on collection tiles, and one hero moment (a slow drift on the hero photo, or a scrubbed rotation of the three loose-diamond renders already on the site). No scroll hijacking, and reduced-motion is respected.

07

Confirm with Romi before copy is final

  1. Founding story. 1969, 1995 or 1999, and what "second generation" refers to.
  2. Sales claims. "$30 million sold", "1000+ clients and retailers", and the named retailers Birks, European Jewellery, Calgary Jewellery.
  3. Memberships still current. Diamond Bourse of Canada founding member, CJA, Israel Diamond Exchange, Rapaport.
  4. Watches. Whether Bespoke sells Rolex, Cartier or Blancpain, or only diamond-set custom watches. The page may need reframing.
  5. Programme terms. Lifetime upgrade exclusions, and the Temporary Ring (3 days, 2.5 mm yellow or white gold Tiffany-style mount, credit toward the final mounting).
  6. Location wording. Thornhill or Vaughan, showroom or office, days and hours.
  7. Google Business profile link and permission to show the live rating.
  8. Accounts. Podium, LeadConnector and Google Ads stay as they are.
  9. Natural versus lab-grown positioning, since both are offered but only natural qualifies for upgrades.
08

Build plan

Kim Consultant standard stack: React 19, Vite, TypeScript strict, Tailwind and Motion on the front; Express on Railway; Cloudflare DNS. The site is static content plus third-party embeds, so there is no database to run.

  1. Discovery (done). Crawl, inventory, this plan.
  2. Foundations. Repo scaffold via the KC build ritual, design tokens, type scale, layout shell, header and footer, mobile action bar. Output: a styleguide page.
  3. Pages. Home, About, Services, the collections template, Booking, Contact, Privacy. Output: a staging URL to review.
  4. Integrations. Podium, LeadConnector, form relay, Google Ads tag, Vimeo, page titles and descriptions, JewelryStore structured data, sitemap, redirect map.
  5. Content and photos. Corrected copy signed off; replacement product photos dropped into the data files.
  6. QA and launch. Playwright, axe and Lighthouse pass, asset cleanup, DNS cutover, Search Console.

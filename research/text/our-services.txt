## Speak to a GIA-Certified Gemologist 
 We don’t just buy and sell gems and jewellery but also help you check the quality and worth of the gems you have in hand.  In order to learn more about how diamonds or gems are graded or inquire about the quality of a particular gemstone, we will be glad to connect you with one of our GIA-certified gemologists.  GIA is the leading authority on gemstones in the world. And their certified gemologists specialize in identifying, grading, and appraising gemstones. They can easily recognize a gemstone's variety and can identify and evaluate details that are not noticeable or visible to the human eye.  So if you’re looking for the ‘RIGHT APPRAISAL’ schedule a call with us today!

## Make a Special Request
 The jewellery designers at Bespoke Diamonds also fulfill special requests made by our clients. These custom requests include:  - Getting ethical "conflict free" origin diamonds, colour gemstones, and gold that are not shown on our website. - Getting alternative metals such as 10K, 14K, 18K, 19K gold, platinum, nickel, and more. - Customizing and repairing diamonds and colour gems like sapphires, emeralds, rubies, etc. Additionally, you can also make modifications to any of our standard styles to fit your preferences. These modifications include: - Changing jewellery finish from polish to matte etc. - Adding extra finishing details e.g. milgrain and hand engraving. - Ring sizing. - Setting different diamond or colour gem accents. - Choosing from natural or lab-grown diamonds. - We also offer custom designs so you can design a true one-of-a-kind piece that will be exclusive to you.
